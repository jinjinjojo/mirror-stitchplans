US and UK terms

US and UK crochet terms converter

The same names mean different stitches: a US double crochet is a UK treble. Paste a pattern and convert all of it at once, with every change marked.

How to tell which a pattern uses: a US pattern never has a “single crochet” in UK terms, so if you see sc it's US. A UK pattern has no sc; it starts with dc where a US one would say sc. Chain (ch) is the same in both.

How it converts

It reads the text once, longest names first, so a US “dc” becomes a UK “tr” and is never turned again into “dtr”. Capitals are kept (SC becomes DC). Words it doesn't know are left alone. Verified Every build converts sample patterns both ways and checks the round trip.
