Refunds

Last updated 2026-10-23.

What this covers

Paid features on Stitchplans: purchases and unlocks, memberships, tips and gifts, and deals between people on the site. Card payments are taken by Stripe.

If a payment goes wrong

If you were charged by mistake, charged twice, or didn't get what you paid for, write to us at the Report a problem link at the bottom of every page with the date and what you bought. We'll fix it or refund you.

Deals between people

  • Money for a deal is held until the work or item is delivered, and is released to the seller about a week after delivery unless there's a problem.
  • A buyer can ask for a refund from the deal page. For a sale or bundle that has already been released, that's possible for 14 days after release.
  • The seller can agree to the refund or dispute it. If it's agreed, a card payment goes back to the card and store credit goes back as store credit. If it's disputed, we look at what both sides say.

Memberships

You can cancel a membership at any time. It stays active until the end of the period you paid for and isn't renewed after that.

Tips and gifts

These are voluntary. If you sent one by mistake, ask us and we'll look at it.

How long it takes

A refund is sent as soon as it's agreed. Your bank may take several working days to show it.

Your rights

This page does not limit any rights you have under the law where you live.
