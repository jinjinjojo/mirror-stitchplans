Size charts

Head, foot and hand size charts

The Craft Yarn Council's standard head, foot and hand measurements, with a column that turns each one into stitches at your own gauge.

You'll need your stitch gauge (per 4 in or 10 cm)

Your gauge

Read it from a washed, blocked swatch on the gauge page. The stitch counts in the tables follow it. The stitches are for the measurement with no ease: hats, socks and mittens fit snugly, so take off 5 to 10 percent, or use the sizing page to set your ease.
