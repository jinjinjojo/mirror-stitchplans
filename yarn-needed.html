Yarn needed

How much yarn do I need?

The most accurate way to estimate yarn at home is to weigh a swatch and scale it up by area. Or swap a pattern's yarn for yours, or see how much is left on a part ball.

You'll need kitchen scales, the ball band

Weigh the swatch on kitchen scales. For a sweater, add up the pieces (front, back, two sleeves) as rectangles.

Why weighing works

A swatch knitted or crocheted at your gauge, in your stitch pattern, already has your tension, your stitch and your yarn's weight built in. Grams per square inch stays the same across the piece, so the whole thing weighs its area divided by the swatch's area, times the swatch's weight. Generic charts by garment size can't know if you work cables or lace. Shaping, seams and fringe add a little, so round up to whole balls and buy one more from the same dye lot.

Need the swatch gauge too? Read it on the gauge page.

Formulas

Grams = swatch grams × piece area ÷ swatch area. Length = grams × ball length ÷ ball weight. Balls = grams ÷ ball weight, rounded up. 1 yard = 0.9144 metres.
